THE ROLE
FDE closes the gap between a promising AI idea and a result the customer can operate, trust and accept.

The team remains accountable across problem definition, system integration, frontline use and evidence of value.

WHY FDE

One team owns the uncertainty across the handoffs.

CONVENTIONAL DELIVERY

Each function completes its stage

  • The problem is translated repeatedly between teams
  • Business assumptions arrive late in engineering
  • Adoption and acceptance become downstream activities
FORWARD-DEPLOYED POD

One accountable team stays with the result

  • Business value, workflow and acceptance are designed together
  • Engineering happens inside the real operating constraints
  • Usage, exceptions and customer feedback change the product quickly
