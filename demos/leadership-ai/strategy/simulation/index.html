Direct AI judgment

Avoid blanket austerity. Bridge the gap with reserves and debt headroom while gating low-multiplier capital projects and protecting services and employment.

Current policy baseline: AED 360M conditional release: Proposed

Key tradeoffs

  • Reserve use smooths the gap but weakens the next-shock buffer.
  • More debt headroom reduces near-term project deferrals but raises debt and servicing pressure.
  • Protecting priority sectors avoids direct cuts but adds fiscal cost and shifts pressure elsewhere.
  • Capital rephasing lowers exposure but slows non-oil growth.
  • Non-oil revenue supports the budget but can suppress private demand.
