THE CIO QUESTION
The investment team does not need more documents. It needs a verifiable decision mechanism.

Investor AI is designed to move from reading material to testing the investment thesis, identifying decision gaps and producing evidence-linked recommendations for human review.

ONE INVESTMENT PATH

From source evidence to transaction action.

Follow a representative later-stage transaction through governed evidence, specialist diligence and investment committee challenge.

RISK TO ACTION

Risk matters when it changes price, terms or the decision.

The system links each claim to evidence, tests its significance and supports investment professionals in translating risk into valuation impact, protective terms, conditional approval, repricing or rejection.

Risk evidence→Valuation impact→Protective terms→Human decision

Investor AI augments professional judgment while fiduciary, legal and investment authority remain with the authorized people and committees.

INVESTMENT ONTOLOGY

Unstructured material becomes an evidence network.

Investor AI reconstructs investment materials as linked entities, claims, assumptions, risks and source anchors rather than relying on document retrieval alone.

Evidence anchors

Source, page, excerpt and confidence

Every material claim retains a path back to the approved source and its applicable scope.

Investment objects

Company, market, financials, technology, ownership and exit

Specialist agents reason over a common investment vocabulary rather than isolated summaries.

Decision relationships

Risk, valuation, terms and return

The ontology makes the decision chain inspectable and preserves disagreement or missing evidence.

Governance

Version, authority and human review

Recommendations remain decision support until the authorized investment committee acts.

SPECIALIST AI TEAM

Different analytical roles. One evidence base.

Specialized agents apply distinct review criteria; they do not automatically constitute independent judgment. A separately configured challenge path tests assumptions before the recommendation reaches the committee.
