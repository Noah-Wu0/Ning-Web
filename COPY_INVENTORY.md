- `h1` Oil & Gas Export Corridors
- `p` Of the 99.6 Mt produced in 2025, 78.7 Mt left the country. A single pipeline — the CPC to Novorossiysk — carried 64.8 Mt of it, and grew 18% in the process.
- `p` Output has since fallen to 45.7 Mt in H1 2026, 91.6% of last year. Deferring the Kashagan turnaround cut the shortfall from 4.9 Mt to 3.3 Mt.
- `p` Kazakh crude only; rail carries under 0.1 Mt, and the Atasu–Alashankou line also moves Russian transit. CPC and Atyrau–Samara both cross Russia: together, 96% of exports.
- `h1` The Reach of Kazakh Energy Exports
- `p` Italy and China each bought close to a fifth of everything Kazakhstan sold abroad in 2025 — two customers, in opposite directions, on either end of the same resource base.
- `p` Energy exports are both the main vehicle of Kazakhstan's external influence and its main surface of external dependency.
- `p` Share of total goods exports, %, January–December 2025. Türkiye (4.9%) and Uzbekistan (4.5%) follow.
- `p` Kazakh crude delivered to Germany through the Druzhba system, Mt — up 44% in 2025. Westward volumes over the Caspian remain small: 1.3 Mt moved through Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan. Gas transit across Kazakhstan is planned to rise from 70.4 bcm in 2025 to 73.3 bcm in 2026.
- `h1` Domestic Operations · H1 2026
- `p` Generation reached 63.4 TWh in the first half of 2026, and renewable output rose 21.2% to 5.1 TWh. About 2.66 GW is still due for commissioning this year, and the Ministry expects the deficit to close by the end of Q1 2027.
- `p` In GW. Kazakhstan reports 27.1 GW installed across 254 energy sources. The 2.66 GW value is a 2026 commissioning plan, not completed capacity.
- `p` Planned capacity is not commissioned capacity. Of the 13.3 GW due by 2029, 7.4 GW is baseload and 5.9 GW renewable, including 7.8 GW from the coal power national project. Gasification reached 64.2% on 1 January 2026, covering 13.1 million people, against 80% by 2035.
- `h1` Smart Metering and the AI Layer
- `p` 2026 is Kazakhstan's Year of Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence. 49,000 smart gas meters are installed nationwide; 1,352,000 are planned by 2029. Each meter is a sensor, and together they form the measurement layer that AI-based forecasting and loss detection depend on.
- `p` Replacement is free to households and funded by the gas supply operator QazaqGaz Aimaq. Readings can now be filed through eGov Mobile, where AI checks the photographed dial against the figure entered — a pilot running in all three cities of republican status and 17 regions until May 2027.
- `p` Digital twins of the Unified Energy System are being built through 2026, on national compute: Alem.Cloud and AL Farabium both rank in the global TOP-500. Energy sits inside the Digital Qazaqstan framework, which pools sector data lakes and a single national register of AI projects.

The mistake many organizations make is trying to rebuild the entire enterprise before proving value.
